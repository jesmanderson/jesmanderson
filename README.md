## 🏗️ Architecture Mindset

As I progress from engineering toward architecture, I try to approach systems by asking:

1. What business problem are we solving?
2. What are the functional and non-functional requirements?
3. What does the current state look like?
4. What dependencies exist?
5. Where are the trust boundaries?
6. What can fail?
7. What are the security risks?
8. What design alternatives exist?
9. What should the future state look like?
10. How will the solution be secured?
11. How will it be monitored?
12. How will it fail over?
13. How will it recover?
14. What can safely be automated?
15. How will success be measured?

### My Engineering Lifecycle

**Design → Secure → Automate → Observe → Recover → Improve**
